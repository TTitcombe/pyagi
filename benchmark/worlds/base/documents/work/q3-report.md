# Northwind Analytics — Q3 2026 Report

## Headline numbers
- Revenue: £4.2m, up 18% quarter on quarter (Q2: £3.56m)
- Gross margin: 61% (Q2: 58%)
- New enterprise customers: 7 (target was 5)
- Net revenue retention: 112%
- Headcount: 54 (+4)

## Highlights
- Closed the Halden Logistics deal, our largest contract to date (£600k ARR).
- Launched the forecasting module; 40% of customers activated it within 6 weeks.

## Risks
- Two of the top ten customers are up for renewal in Q4, together 19% of revenue.
- Hiring for senior data engineers is running about 2 months behind plan.
- Cloud costs rose 23%, faster than revenue; a cost review is under way.

## Q4 priorities
1. Secure both top-ten renewals.
2. Launch Project Atlas (self-serve analytics) in beta.
3. Bring cloud cost growth below revenue growth.
